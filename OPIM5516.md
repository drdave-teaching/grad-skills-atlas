# OPIM 5516 — Skills Atlas (Advanced Deep Learning)

*What you'll be able to do — each skill paired with its theory, its real-world implementation, and **how to practice it**. Taught in **Python / Keras / PyTorch** on Colab + GCP.*

**Legend:** ✋ **by hand** (math/manual) · 💻 **local code** (a notebook) · ☁️ **production** (deployed in the cloud)

**Learning objectives:** ①quantify **uncertainty** in a neural net ②model **relational/graph** data ③build **transformers** for sequence, text, image & video ④source real data via **cloud web-scraping** ⑤ship a **spatiotemporal forecasting** project

---

## In one sentence
By the end, **you can build neural networks that know what they don't know (uncertainty), that reason over graphs (recommenders, traffic), and that use the transformer architecture across time series, text, images, and video** — the current frontier, applied end to end.
