# OPIM 5502 — Skills Atlas (Big Data Analytics with Cloud Computing)

*What you'll be able to do — each skill paired with its theory, its real-world implementation, and **how to practice it**. Taught in **PySpark** on Google Colab & Databricks.*

**Legend:** ✋ **by hand** (math/manual) · 💻 **local code** (a notebook) · ☁️ **production** (deployed/distributed)

**Learning objectives:** ①work at the **command line** & understand distributed compute ②wrangle big **DataFrames** (EDA, joins, SQL) ③parse **complex/nested data** (JSON) ④drop to **RDDs & UDFs** ⑤use **window functions** ⑥build **ML pipelines** with Spark MLlib ⑦scale to a **cluster / HPC**

---

## In one sentence
By the end, **you can take a data set too big for one machine, wrangle and query it in PySpark (DataFrames, SQL, JSON, window functions), extend Spark with your own UDFs, and train an ML pipeline that scales from Colab to a cluster.**

## In the curriculum
5502 is the **"make it big" specialization.** It re-teaches the shared core — EDA, joins, the ML pipeline, cross-validation — but in the **distributed** idiom, so students see that the *methodology* is invariant while the *engine* changes (pandas → Spark, sklearn `Pipeline` → MLlib `Pipeline`). Its cloud/cluster thread (Databricks, HPC) is a sibling of **5512**'s GCP production module: two flavors of "beyond your laptop."
