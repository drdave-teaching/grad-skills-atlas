# OPIM 5603 — Skills Atlas (Statistics in Business Analytics)

*What you'll be able to do — each skill paired with its theory, its real-world implementation, and **how to practice it**. Taught in **R** (via Google Colab).*

**Legend:** ✋ **by hand** (math/manual) · 💻 **local code** (a notebook) · ☁️ **production** (deployed in the cloud)

**Learning objectives:** ①work in **R** and describe data ②reason with **probability distributions** ③use **sampling, the CLT & confidence intervals** ④run a **hypothesis test** end to end ⑤understand estimation via **maximum likelihood** ⑥fit & interpret **regression / GLMs** ⑦analyze **categorical data**

---

## In one sentence
By the end, **you can describe data, quantify uncertainty with distributions and confidence intervals, run a hypothesis test the standard five-step way, and fit and interpret a regression — understanding where every number comes from, from the likelihood up.**

It's the **only R course** — everything else is Python — so it also builds language-transfer confidence.
