# OPIM 3510 — Skills Atlas (Business Data Analytics I)

### *Data Storytelling, Applied Stats & Geospatial* · undergraduate

*What you'll be able to do — each skill paired with its theory, its real-world implementation, and **how to practice it**. Taught in **Python / Google Colab**.*

**Legend:** ✋ **by hand** (math/manual) · 💻 **local code** (a notebook) · ☁️ **production** (deployed in the cloud)

**Learning objectives:** ①write practical **Python** ②wrangle data with **pandas** (merge/join/query) ③reason with **applied statistics** (sampling, CLT, CIs) ④make **maps** with GeoPandas ⑤**forecast** a time series (moving averages, exponential smoothing)

---

## In one sentence
By the end, **you are data-competent: you can read, merge, and describe real data, run the statistics that say whether things differ, make a map that tells a story, and forecast a time series — the whole descriptive-analytics toolkit, in Python.**
